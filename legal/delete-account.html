Delete your GK Swal account

GK Swal users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

In the app

  1. Open GK Swal and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose Delete my account and data.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Delete permanently.

The app uses a secure Firebase callable service to remove the user's account, profile, authored posts, related community data, submitted reports, wallet ledger, stored UPI withdrawal requests, submitted reward claims, quiz sessions, referral records and local learning data on that device. A pending withdrawal will not be paid after its request is deleted; resolve any pending payment before deleting the account.

Data that may remain

Data that must be retained for legal, security or abuse-prevention reasons should be identified in the final privacy policy, with its retention period. Do not publish this page until the actual deletion behaviour has been verified.
