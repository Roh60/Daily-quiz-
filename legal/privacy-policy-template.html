GK Swal Privacy Policy

Publication draft — verify every statement against the final production build, fill the developer contact details, and publish at a stable public HTTPS URL before Google Play submission.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Information we process

GK Swal may process account identifiers and profile information supplied by the sign-in provider; text posts, comments, poll votes and reports you submit; and quiz progress/bookmarks stored on your device. If Firebase services are enabled, relevant account and community data may be stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Wallet and UPI withdrawal requests

If the wallet feature is enabled, we process wallet balance and transaction records. When you submit a withdrawal request, we collect the UPI ID you provide, requested amount and request status. If the operator enables a payout provider such as RazorpayX, the UPI ID, amount and necessary transaction metadata may be sent to that provider to process the payout. Payouts are not guaranteed to be instant; the request status must be checked against the provider result. Do not add funds or promise rewards unless a legitimate reward program and eligibility rules have been published. Verify payment-provider and applicable legal retention requirements before production.

Verified quiz rewards and referrals

If server-verified reward quizzes or referral rewards are enabled, we process quiz session IDs, submitted answers, score, timing, referral codes, eligibility status, reward decisions and wallet ledger entries to prevent fraud and calculate eligible rewards. Rewards are not guaranteed and may be subject to published eligibility limits and review.

Currency conversion

If you use the currency converter, the amount and currency codes are sent to a reference-rate service to calculate an indicative conversion. This is not a bank quote or a payout exchange rate.

Purpose

We use this information to provide sign-in, quizzes, bookmarks, learning history, community features, wallet accounting and withdrawal requests, abuse reporting, security and support.

Consent choices

The app includes Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. Google may process consent-related signals and device/app information to display the consent form and remember the user’s choices. A privacy-options control is shown when UMP indicates that it is required. Verify this wording against the final production configuration and applicable regional requirements.

Advertising

The source project includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) integration. Whether ads are served depends on the final production configuration and consent state. The app requests consent information before allowing ad requests. Debug builds use Google sample test ad identifiers. Production banner ads are shown only when the developer configures a real AdMob banner ad-unit ID and UMP indicates that ad requests are permitted. If production ads are enabled, Google may process device identifiers, diagnostics and ad interaction data under Google's policies. The developer must complete the Google Play Data safety form and verify this disclosure against the final production configuration before publishing.

Sharing and service providers

Data may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, including Firebase and, if enabled, Google Mobile Ads. Do not claim that data is never shared until the production SDKs and configuration have been audited.

Retention and deletion

You may request account deletion using the in-app account deletion option when available, or contact the developer using the contact details published below. The deletion workflow is designed to remove the user wallet ledger, withdrawal request documents (including stored UPI ID), submitted reward claims, quiz sessions and referral records associated with the account. Verify this against the deployed production database and any applicable legal/accounting retention duties before publication. Complete the actual deletion workflow before publishing this policy.

Children

GK Swal is intended for general exam-preparation users. The developer must set the target audience accurately in Google Play Console and apply any child-safety requirements that apply.

Security

We use access controls and service-provider security features appropriate to the deployed configuration. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
